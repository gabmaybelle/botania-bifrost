# Changelog

Botania Bifrost is an unofficial port of Botania by Vazkii and the Botania contributors to Minecraft 26.3.

## 1.0.0-beta.3

- The Lexica Botania opens its book again. It now needs Patchouli: install the unofficial 26.3 port of Patchouli,
  version 26.3-94+port.2 or newer, on the client and the server. Older versions show some entry names blank.
- The book's recipe, structure and Botania pages all draw, search and bookmarks work, and Visualize shows a
  structure's ghost blocks in the world.
- Fixed: text on Botania's on-screen displays was invisible. Block names on the Wand of the Forest display, the
  readouts of functional flowers, sparks and the Hovering Hourglass, the Gaia Guardian's name on its health bar
  and the mana tier labels in tooltips show again.

## 1.0.0-beta.2

- Mana, Natura and Gaia Pylons give an enchanting table its power again on Fabric (they counted for nothing in
  beta.1). NeoForge was already right.

## 1.0.0-beta.1

The first release of the port, for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric and NeoForge.

- Botania's flowers, mana, items, blocks, the Alfheim portal, the Gaia Guardian, Corporea and Garden of Glass work
  on 26.3. Botania's own game tests all pass on both loaders.
- Rings, amulets and belts go in accessory slots: Trinkets Updated on Fabric, Curios on NeoForge (both optional).
- JEI and REI show Botania's recipes.
- The Lexica Botania can't open its book yet, because Patchouli has no 26.3 version. Until it does, using the
  Lexica points you to the online Lexicon.
- Worlds from Botania's 1.21.1 port load in 26.3: items inside Botania blocks, entities and items keep their
  names and enchantments, force relay pairs and Garden of Glass islands carry over.
- Worlds from Botania for 1.20.1 are not supported yet. Back up any world before opening it with this port.
